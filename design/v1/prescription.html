Pupillary distance (PD)

The distance between your pupils, in millimetres. It is often written as one number, sometimes as two.

What do these numbers mean?
Sphere (SPH)
The main strength. A minus number corrects short sight, a plus number long sight.
Cylinder (CYL) and Axis
Correct astigmatism. They always come as a pair: if there is a cylinder, there is an axis between 1 and 180.
Add
Extra strength for reading, used in progressive and reading lenses. Usually the same for both eyes.
PD
Centres the lenses on your eyes. If it is not on your prescription, your optician can give it to you.
